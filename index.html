LGA Lingo & WHS Handicap Rules

The Green Jacket & Tour Glory

Awarded annually to the LGA Championship winner over 36 holes of Stableford competition. Current holder: James Patrick (Reigning Champion).

WHS Score Differential Formula

(113 ÷ Slope) × (Adjusted Gross − Scratch Rating − PCC)

Your WHS Handicap Index averages the lowest 8 differentials from your most recent 20 rounds.

Daily Playing Handicap

Round(Index × (Slope ÷ 113) + (CR − Par))

Determines how many Stableford handicap strokes you receive on the day based on each hole's Stroke Index (SI 1–18).
