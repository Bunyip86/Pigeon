Tour Standards & Local Conditions

LGA Rules

Core playing rules enforced across all Legends Golfing Association rounds and Pidgeon Cup fixtures.

Putting Policy

No Gimmies

Every putt must be holed out regardless of distance—no tap-ins or conceded putts are permitted in stroke or Stableford scoring unless a hole is already conceded in match play.

Relief Procedure

Ball Drops & Relief Areas

Drop the ball straight down from knee height into the designated relief area without touching any part of the course on the way down. If the dropped ball rolls outside the relief area, re-drop once; if it rolls outside a second time, place it where it first struck the ground on the second drop.

Penalty Areas

Water & Penalty Areas (Red & Yellow)

You may play the ball as it lies or take one penalty stroke for: (a) stroke-and-distance from the previous spot, or (b) back-on-the-line relief keeping the flagstick between you and the hole. For red penalty areas only, you may also take two club-lengths lateral relief from the estimated entry point, no nearer the hole.

Unplayable Lie & OB

Unplayable Balls, Out of Bounds & Provisionals

You are the sole judge of an unplayable lie and may take one penalty stroke using stroke-and-distance, back-on-the-line, or two club-lengths lateral relief. Out of bounds carries stroke-and-distance; always announce and play a provisional ball before walking forward if a ball may be lost outside a penalty area or out of bounds.
